# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards
- Use welcoming, respectful, and inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Show empathy and courtesy toward all participants.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing private information without explicit consent are not tolerated.
